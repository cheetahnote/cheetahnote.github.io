Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Cheetah Note?

No account or sign-up is required. You can start creating notes immediately upon downloading.

How does note synchronization work?

Sync is powered by your personal Apple iCloud account. Your notes stay encrypted and sync across all your devices automatically.

Is Cheetah Note free?

Yes, Cheetah Note is free to download and use on the App Store.

Can I restore accidentally edited or deleted text?

Yes. Cheetah Note includes an editing history feature that lets you inspect and restore past revisions of your notes.

How is Cheetah Note different from Apple Notes or heavy workspace apps?

Unlike complex workspace apps or multi-folder editors, Cheetah Note is built purely for speed: it opens instantly to a distraction-free plain-text pad, pins memos directly to your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch, and includes built-in editing revision history so you never lose accidental edits.
